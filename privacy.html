Privacy policy

Chuckmium Publisher · last updated 23 November 2026

Who we are

Chuckmium Publisher ("the app") is a private publishing tool operated by Chuckmium. It is used only by Chuckmium to publish Chuckmium's own videos to Chuckmium's own accounts. Contact: chuckmium@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): with the account owner's permission, the app uploads videos to the owner's channel (scope youtube.upload) and reads the channel's name, subscriber count and the view, like and comment counts of the channel's own videos (scope youtube.readonly).
  • Facebook (Meta Graph API): publishes Reels to the Chuckmium Page and reads the Page's follower count and the play counts of its own Reels.
  • TikTok (Content Posting API), if connected: publishes videos to the Chuckmium account and reads basic account information needed to post.

The app does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other users. It does not read comments, messages, contacts or audience demographics.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and the statistics above are stored only on Chuckmium's own computer, in files readable only by the account owner, and are used only to publish Chuckmium's videos and to see how they perform. Statistics are kept only as long as Chuckmium needs them to judge its videos, and are deleted on request. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be withdrawn at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for Facebook in Business Integrations settings, and for TikTok under Manage app permissions. When access is withdrawn the stored token stops working and the app deletes it the next time it runs; to have stored statistics deleted, email chuckmium@gmail.com and they are deleted within 7 days.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
