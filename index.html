Chuckmium

Chuckmium makes short videos about technology news: one story per video, under a minute, with the sources credited in every description.

Each video is written from reporting by established outlets, checked against those reports, narrated with an AI voice and illustrated with openly licensed photographs (credited in the description). Videos are labelled as made with AI where the platform offers a label.

  • TikTok: @chuckmium

Chuckmium Publisher

Chuckmium Publisher is our own publishing tool. It uploads Chuckmium's finished videos to Chuckmium's own accounts through each platform's official API (YouTube Data API, Meta Graph API, TikTok Content Posting API) and reads the view counts of those videos. It is used only by Chuckmium, for Chuckmium's accounts. See the privacy policy for what data it uses.
